## Evidence and scope

### ClickUp

Scope: Direct ClickUp paid workspace subscription only. Not merchandise, marketplace purchases or a general consumer-return rule.

The official page describes role-based software subscription fees and an initial paid-plan refund guarantee. This supports a subscription/refund scope, not a physical-goods return decision. [Official source](https://help.clickup.com/hc/en-us/articles/6303244318999-Pricing-per-user-role-and-plan)

### Miro

Scope: Direct Miro Starter or Business subscription only. Excludes marketplace apps and customer transactions.

The official article covers administrative billing actions, subscription cancellation and support review of incorrect charges. It does not establish a blanket merchandise or digital-product return entitlement. [Official source](https://help.miro.com/hc/en-us/articles/360017571694-Manage-your-subscription)

### monday.com

Scope: Direct monday.com software account subscription only. Excludes marketplace apps, separately negotiated terms and downstream customer sales.

The official policy distinguishes first-time purchases, renewed accounts and seat auto-upgrades. It describes subscription refunds and cancellation, not a separate physical-return operation. [Official source](https://support.monday.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005320069-Refund-eligibility)

### Typeform

Scope: Direct Typeform paid subscription only. Excludes respondent payments collected through forms and third-party products.

The official article discusses downgrading paid features, prepaid billing periods and refunds for canceled plans. A separate goods-return decision is not established for this scoped subscription. [Official source](https://help.typeform.com/hc/en-us/articles/360038887692-What-happens-if-I-cancel-my-paid-account)

### Vimeo

Scope: Direct Vimeo platform subscription only. Excludes Vimeo OTT creator sales, viewer purchases, add-ons and app-store billing.

The official article separates monthly and annual initial subscriptions, app-store purchases and add-ons. Its subject is platform subscription refunds, not a generic return right across Vimeo products. [Official source](https://help.vimeo.com/hc/en-us/articles/12425462316433-FAQ-Refunds-for-Vimeo-subscriptions)

### Skillshare

Scope: Membership subscription, digital-product purchase and one-to-one session must be separate scopes.

The official policy expressly addresses non-returnable digital products separately from membership refunds and session disputes. A vendor-wide 'return not applicable' approval would erase a real product distinction. [Official source](https://help.skillshare.com/hc/en-us/articles/204536798-Can-I-get-a-refund-for-my-Skillshare-subscription-or-purchase)

### Thinkific

Scope: Creator's direct Thinkific platform subscription only. Never substitute this for a learner's purchase from a course creator.

The tracked refund source is a 2026 pricing-change article. It discusses cancellation and jurisdiction-dependent annual-term refunds but is not sufficient evidence for a general return or refund policy. [Official source](https://support.thinkific.com/hc/en-us/articles/41582716364567-Thinkific-s-2026-Plan-Pricing-Update-What-It-Means-for-You)
